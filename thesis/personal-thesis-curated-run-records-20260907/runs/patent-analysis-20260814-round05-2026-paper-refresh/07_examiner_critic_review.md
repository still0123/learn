# Adversarial Examiner Review

## Attack 1: Active acquisition is known

AVP, Video-MTR, Video-o3, Chain-of-Glimpse and EviSelect collectively teach query-driven video search, temporal/spatial actions, cost-aware selection and stopping.

**Response:** Do not argue novelty from active acquisition. Argue the full relation from answer-independent QSlot resolution gain to audited evidence and restricted patch commit.

## Attack 2: Verification authority is known

VideoSEAL teaches planner/inspector separation; Kestrel teaches evidence-verified refinement.

**Response:** Authority separation alone is not relied upon. The planner metadata is excluded from evidence, and only audited records can enter fixed-candidate `D/M`, which controls `P subset W`.

## Attack 3: Counterfactual attribution is known

CREDiT, VAD and related work teach evidence-present/evidence-removed comparisons.

**Response:** `D` alone is conceded as high risk. It must remain coupled to same-evidence old/new value advantage `M`, `W\\P` support and atomic rollback.

## Attack 4: Claims and citations are known

CRAFT and TRACE teach atomic claims, evidence citations, critic repair and claim consolidation.

**Response:** The claimed relation is not claim extraction. It is a pre-frozen program write set whose nonmembers are copied from `V0` and whose members are atomically committed only after evidence gates.

## Attack 5: Software transaction analogy

`W/P`, commit and rollback can be characterized as applying database transaction concepts to text generation.

**Response:** The transaction boundary is generated from video-evidence dependencies; admission depends on raw-video command provenance, fixed-candidate evidence dependence and same-slot value advantage. Additional patent searching is still required to determine whether this coupling is obvious.

## Verdict

The proposal survives only as a tightly coupled candidate. It does not support a guarantee of novelty or inventive step.
