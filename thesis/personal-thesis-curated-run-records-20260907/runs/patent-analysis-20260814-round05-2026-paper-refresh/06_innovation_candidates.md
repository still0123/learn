# Innovation Candidates

## Candidate 1: QSlot Resolution-Gain Acquisition

- Mechanism: predict whether an action reduces a question-conditioned evidence gap or makes a slot resolvable, including reliable refutation of the old value.
- Non-combination relation: the prediction target is independent of the old answer value and controls an executable video command.
- Risk: information gain and active perception are generally known.
- Decision: retain only as the acquisition half of the complete transaction.

## Candidate 2: Non-Evidentiary Planning Boundary

- Mechanism: action descriptors, scores and commands are control metadata and cannot support a candidate claim.
- Non-combination relation: only audited raw-video-derived records can enter `D/M/K`.
- Risk: authority decoupling is known from VideoSEAL; data-admission detail may be considered engineering practice.
- Decision: retain in the independent claim as an integrity constraint, not as a standalone invention.

## Candidate 3: Audited Claim Patch Transaction

- Mechanism: audited evidence bound to `claim_id` generates `P subset W`; `D/M/K` and `W\\P` support jointly gate atomic commit or restore.
- Non-combination relation: acquisition output directly controls a bounded program write set through evidence-specific gates.
- Risk: examiner may combine evidence verification with software transactions.
- Decision: strongest surviving core; retain.

## Rejected Additions

- MCTS, GRPO, multi-agent planners, fixed seek/zoom actions, dynamic resolution, temporal reversal and generic critic loops.
- Reason: heavily disclosed in 2026 papers and unnecessary to complete the current control chain.
