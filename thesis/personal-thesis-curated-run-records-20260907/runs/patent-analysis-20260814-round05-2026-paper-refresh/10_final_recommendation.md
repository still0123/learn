## Decision

```text
QSlot resolution-gain acquisition
→ executable command
```

## Most Important Revision

The selector must optimize slot resolvability, not support for the old answer value. Otherwise it systematically prefers confirming evidence and can miss corrective evidence.

2. Only command-consistent, budget-compliant and traceable acquisition output enters evidence gates.
3. `D/M/K` controls a program-level claim write set, not free-form answer regeneration.
4. Any failed gate restores the complete prior answer version.
